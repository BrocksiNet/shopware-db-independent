A Shopware experiment

What if we just
threw out the database?

And then the DAL too.
Being a bit crazy, on purpose, to find what really slows us down.
The idea

Two slightly crazy questions

1
Can Shopware run on PostgreSQL and SQLite, not just MySQL, without a rewrite?
2
Do we even need our own DAL? Would Doctrine, Cycle ORM or plain SQL do better?
Nobody planned to ship this. The goal: break things and see where it hurts.
How we ran it

Small bets, fast waves, hard numbers

  • 8 waves in 6 days (Nov 18 – 23)
  • AI agents in parallel lanes, every wave split into handoffs
  • Every claim backed by a measurement: 4 databases, same machine, same data
  • 118 commits on the proof-of-concept branch, 479 files touched

The verdict

Keep the DAL. Make it better.

Replace it with an ORM

  • 100 – 230 person-weeks (estimate)
  • breaks every plugin and app
  • no translations, inheritance, versioning out of the box
  • still one statement per row on writes

Build DAL v2 step by step

  • 11 – 27 person-weeks (estimate)
  • no breaking change
  • every lever measured on 4 databases
  • next target: the write path

Why experiments matter now

What we take away

  • Crazy questions got cheap. With AI agents, a big what-if fits into a week.
  • Removing something shows the bottleneck. Without the DAL we saw 371 statements.
  • A "no" is a result. We won't replace the DAL, and now we know why, in numbers.
  • Side findings pay off right away. 34 fixes for today's MySQL setup.
  • Measure, don't guess. Our intuition was wrong more than once.
